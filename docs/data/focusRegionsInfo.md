# Focus Regions

Focus Regions make a selected atom, molecule, or defect easier to inspect in a
large structure. Select atoms in the scene or Structure panel and choose **Add
from selection**.

Atoms in the inner sphere retain local structural context. The outer shell can
be made more transparent, and atoms beyond it can be faded or hidden. Disable
the inner region when the selected group itself is the object of interest.

Regions are non-destructive: their opacity is combined with existing atom
opacity without changing it. Overlapping regions keep the most visible result.
Use **Exclude selection** to keep additional atoms unchanged.
