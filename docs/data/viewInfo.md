# View

Camera and view-direction controls for the 3D scene.

- **x / y / z / a / b / c** buttons snap the camera to look down that Cartesian axis or lattice vector.
- Long-pressing an axis button reveals **▲/▼** step-rotate arrows that turn the view in small steps around that axis. The View window's ≡ menu **Stepwise buttons** section can keep them always visible (**On**), disable them (**Off**), or reveal them on long press (**Long press**, the default).
- **Reset** restores the default framing, centered on the current structure.
- Perspective/Parallel, Damping and Auto-rotation are configured in the **Visual** window's Camera section.
