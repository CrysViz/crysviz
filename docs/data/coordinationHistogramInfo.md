# Coordination Number Histogram

How many bonds start or end on each atom, grouped by element. Click a bar to highlight every atom
of that element with that coordination number in the 3D view — click it again to clear the
highlight.

Opened with **Open** next to *Coordination Number* under **Histograms** in the Bonds window. It
opens in the wide dock on the right of the 3D scene; like any window, it can be dragged out to
float, or into the left bar.
