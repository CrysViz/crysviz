# Polyhedra Connectivity

How neighbouring polyhedra connect to each other:

- **Corner** — share exactly one vertex atom.
- **Edge** — share exactly two vertex atoms.
- **Face** — share three or more vertex atoms.
- **Bond-bridge** — share no vertex, but a vertex atom of one is directly bonded to a vertex
  atom of the other.
- **Trimer-bridge** — share no vertex, but a vertex atom of one reaches a vertex atom of the
  other through exactly one intermediate atom (vertex–bond–atom–bond–vertex), where that atom
  isn't itself the centre of another polyhedron — common in nitride-type networks, where a
  bridging ligand atom links two otherwise-unconnected clusters.

The bar chart groups connection counts by sharing type and polyhedron-pair category; the list
below it shows every individual connection. Click a bar or a list row to highlight the atoms
involved (centres, vertices, and any bridging atom) — click again to clear.
