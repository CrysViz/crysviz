# Bond Length Histogram

One low-profile bar chart per bond species pair (Y-O, Cu-O, Ba-O, ...), plus an **All Pairs**
combined chart (every pair as its own series, legend, the old style) — all in one scrollable list.
Every card starts collapsed to just its header; click its ▾ to expand, or **Expand All**/**Collapse
All** for all of them at once. **Bin width** at the top sets the bar width for every card. Drag a
card's own slider (under its chart) to give just that card a custom view — the ↺ button resets it.
Drag a chart's bottom-right corner to make it taller.

Click a bar to highlight the bonds it represents in the 3D view (click again to clear); hover a
bar to see the actual bonds in that bin. Turn on **Show distances** to also drop a floating Å
label on each bond when its bar is selected.

Also available in the split view beside the 3D scene (see the "Split View" button next to this
one in the Bonds panel).
