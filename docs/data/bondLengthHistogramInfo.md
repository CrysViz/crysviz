# Bond Length Histogram

A live bond-length distribution for every bond pair in the structure, grouped by species pair.
Drag **Bins** to change the bar count and **Max length** to change the plotted range. Click a bar
to highlight the bonds it represents in the 3D view — click it again to clear the highlight.

Also available in the split view beside the 3D scene (see the "Split View" button next to this
one in the Bonds panel).
