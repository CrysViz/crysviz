# Energy Landscape

Explores an energy surface built from a set of related structures (e.g. displacements or scans).
Load a landscape JSON in this window; the heatmaps open automatically in a separate Landscape
Plots window in the wide dock on the right of the 3D scene. Clicking a point in the landscape
jumps the 3D view to the corresponding structure. Like any window, the plots can be dragged out
to float or into the left bar.
