# Polyhedra Volume

The distribution of coordination-polyhedron **volumes** across the structure — the volume of
the convex hull of each polyhedron's vertex atoms (Å³), the standard measure of how large a
coordination environment is.

The bar chart bins the volumes, with one colour series per polyhedron category (BaO₁₂, TiO₆,
…); the **Bin width** slider sets the bin size. The list below it shows every individual
polyhedron, sorted by volume. Click a bar or a list row to highlight the corresponding
polyhedra in the 3D view — click again to clear.

One representative is plotted per physical polyhedron (periodic-image copies share a volume);
the highlight expands each back to all its copies when "Link periodic copies" is on.

Also available from the Polyhedra panel's Histograms section.
